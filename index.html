Conference paper: Evaluating the Role of Next-Generation Battery Materials in Improving the Energy Density, Charging Speed and Performance of Electric Vehicle Batteries

Abstract

      The future will contain many new materials and devices but how will we charge them, and how will they affect the environment around us?
      This paper aims to identify the different new materials and to evaluate the efficiency of them so that we can understand which ones are best for us to use to charge electric cars and next-gen tech.
      Modern lithium-ion batteries have problems such as degradation of the battery, restrictive capacity and the reliance on raw materials.
